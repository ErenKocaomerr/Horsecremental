# MILESTONE 4 — BLACKSMITH, EQUIPMENT & PRODUCTION PROGRESSION

The goal of Milestone 4 is to make Stable progression deeper without making the game complicated.

Add:

**Blacksmith**
+
**Equipment**
+
**Crafting**
+
**Production Upgrades**

The player should now have multiple satisfying ways to improve the horse while Training Races automatically generate resources.

The new core loop becomes:

Training Races

↓

Earn Coins + basic crafting material

↓

Upgrade Farm / Blacksmith

↓

Craft better horse equipment

↓

Horse becomes stronger

↓

Training improves

↓

Attempt difficult Main Race

↓

Progress further

This should still feel like a simple incremental game.

Do NOT turn this into a complicated RPG or inventory management game.

---

# IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

Milestone 4 is about adding **depth to the Stable**, not complexity.

The player should now constantly have something satisfying happening:

Training Race finishing.

Farm producing Carrots.

Blacksmith crafting Equipment.

Coins increasing.

Metal accumulating.

An upgrade becoming affordable.

A new Equipment tier finishing.

Main Race becoming slightly more possible.

This is the feeling we want.

Avoid complicated RPG systems.

Avoid random systems.

Avoid large inventories.

Avoid systems where the player has to read many stats.

Every system should answer a simple question:

**Does this make my horse stronger or make progression faster?**

If not, it probably does not belong in Milestone 4.

The game is still short-scope.

Do not overengineer.

Do not begin Milestone 5 automatically.
