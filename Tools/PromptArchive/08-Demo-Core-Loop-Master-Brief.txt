# NEW DEMO CORE LOOP

The current game is still too passive and idle.

We are changing the core structure.

The demo should now revolve around:

**Stable / Farm**

↓

**Player chooses Training Races whenever they want**

↓

**Training Races reward Coins + Training Tokens depending on finishing position**

↓

**Finishing 1st unlocks the next harder Training Race**

↓

**Player upgrades the horse and Stable**

↓

**Time continuously passes through a Day / Night Cycle**

↓

**A mandatory Main Race happens after a fixed number of in-game days**

↓

**Player must attempt the Main Race**

↓

**Win = progression**

**Lose = return to preparation period and try again later**

The important feeling is:

**"I only have a limited amount of time before the next Main Race. How strong can I make my horse before race day?"**

---

# IMPORTANT DESIGN CHANGE — REMOVE PASSIVE TRAINING HORSE SYSTEM

Remove the current gameplay concept where the player buys 3–4+ Training Horses that automatically run and generate passive income.

We are returning the focus to ONE important player horse.

The fantasy is:

**Take this horse and turn it into a World Champion.**

Do not build the economy around owning many production horses.

Training is now something the PLAYER chooses to actively race.

---

# TRAINING RACES

Training Races are manual races that the player can enter whenever they want.

They use the normal horse racing mechanics:

- Automatic forward movement
- SPACE Boost
- E Jump
- AI opponents
- Obstacles where appropriate
- Finish position

Training Races should be shorter and easier than Main Races.

There should be a Training progression ladder.

Example:

Training I

↓

Finish 1st

↓

Training II unlocked

↓

Finish 1st

↓

Training III unlocked

and so on.

For the demo approximately 5–6 Training Races are enough.

Do NOT build dozens of races.

---

# TRAINING REWARDS

Training Races reward:

**Coins**

and

**Training Tokens**

Rewards depend on finishing position.

Example concept:

1st:
100% Coin Reward
100% Token Reward
Unlock next Training Race

2nd:
75% Coin Reward
65% Token Reward

3rd:
50% Coin Reward
40% Token Reward

4th:
30% Coin Reward
Minimum Token Reward

Exact values should remain configurable.

A bad Training Race should never feel like completely wasted time.

---

# TRAINING TOKENS

Training Tokens must have a distinct purpose.

Do NOT make them simply "Coin Currency #2".

Use:

### COINS

for Stable / production / Farm / infrastructure progression.

### TRAINING TOKENS

for improving the HORSE itself.

Training Tokens should purchase things such as:

- Speed
- Acceleration
- Boost Power
- Possibly Boost Gain
- Possibly Jump Power later

This creates a clear relationship:

**Training → Horse becomes stronger**

and:

**Farm / Economy → Stable becomes stronger**

---

# TRAINING RACE LADDER

Prototype structure can be approximately:

Training I
Training II
Training III
Training IV
Training V
Training VI

Only Training I starts unlocked.

The next Training Race unlocks ONLY when the player finishes 1st in the previous Training Race.

Higher Training tiers should:

- Have stronger AI
- Give more Coins
- Give more Training Tokens

Do NOT dynamically scale old Training opponents to player power.

Old races should become easier as the player becomes stronger.

This is important for incremental power progression.

# DAY / NIGHT CYCLE

Add a continuous Day / Night Cycle.

Time should ALWAYS keep moving during normal gameplay.

There should NOT be:

- Day-end loading screens
- Forced sleep
- Day reset screens
- Scene reset
- Gameplay interruption every night

The world simply transitions visually.

Example:

Morning

↓

Day

↓

Sunset

↓

Night

↓

Sunrise

↓

Next Day

Lighting and world visuals should change gradually.

Gameplay systems do NOT receive stat bonuses or penalties based on time of day.

Day / Night is primarily:

**Visual atmosphere + Main Race scheduling.**

# MANDATORY MAIN RACE

When Main Race time arrives:

The player must attempt the Main Race.

Do not allow the player to infinitely delay it.

However:

If the player is currently inside a Training Race when Main Race time arrives:

DO NOT interrupt the Training Race halfway through.

Allow the Training Race to finish.

Then queue the Main Race immediately afterward.

Prefer the existing unified world.

# MAIN RACE DIFFICULTY

Main Races should be HARD progression gates.

They should test whether the player prepared enough before race day.

A fresh / under-upgraded player should often lose.

The player should think:

"I need stronger Training upgrades and horse stats before the next attempt."

Do NOT use aggressive rubber-banding.

Opponents should have meaningful fixed stats.

Upgrading the horse must actually make future attempts easier.

---

# MAIN RACE FAILURE

For the prototype use this failure philosophy:

DO NOT:

- Remove Horse Stats
- Remove Equipment
- Delete Coins
- Reset progression
- Take away Training unlocks

Failure should NOT undo the player's incremental progress.

The primary punishment is:

**You did not advance.**

After losing:

Return to Stable.

Start a short preparation / recovery period.

Recommended prototype:

**2 in-game Recovery Days**

Then the same Main Race is attempted again.

---

# COMEBACK BONUS

Add a lightweight anti-frustration system.

If the player loses a Main Race:

Until the next Main Race attempt:

Training Token rewards receive a temporary bonus.

Example:

First failure:

+20% Training Token Gain

Second consecutive failure:

+35%

Third+:

Maximum +50%

When the player finally wins the Main Race:

Reset Comeback Bonus to 0%.

Keep values configurable.

This should help the player recover without making the next race automatically free.
