We are continuing development of our short-scope incremental horse racing game.

**Milestones 1, 2, 3 and the current Milestone 4 implementation already exist.**

Before changing anything, inspect the entire current project carefully.

Understand what is already implemented, what systems depend on each other, and what can safely be reused.

Do NOT blindly rewrite working systems.

# MILESTONE 4.5 — MAKE THE STABLE ACTUALLY FUN

The current Stable gameplay is too passive.

Right now too much of the experience is:

Wait

↓

Timer finishes

↓

Collect resource

↓

Buy upgrade

↓

Wait again

This technically works as an incremental game, but it does not feel interactive enough.

The purpose of Milestone 4.5 is to transform the Stable from a mostly UI-driven idle screen into a **living gameplay area where the player constantly sees things happening and can actively interact with the world.**

The player should still earn resources automatically.

However, active interaction should make progression faster and more satisfying.

The Stable should feel like a small incremental playground.

The main systems for this milestone are:

**Visible Training Area**

+

**Training Upgrades**

+

**Interactive Physical Farm**

+

**World Item Drops / Hover Pickup**

+

**Blacksmith temporarily becomes visual-only**

+

**Main Race remains a separate special gameplay area**

Do NOT add another large progression system.

Do NOT add more menus just for the sake of adding content.

This milestone is about improving interaction and game feel.

---

# 1. CORE DESIGN TARGET

The Stable should no longer feel like:

"Watch progress bars."

It should feel like:

**See something happening**

↓

**Interact with it**

↓

**Get immediate feedback**

↓

**Collect something**

↓

**Upgrade something**

↓

**See the world physically improve**

The player should usually have something small they can interact with every few seconds.

The gameplay should remain relaxing and simple.

Do NOT turn the Stable into a difficult skill-based game.

---

# 2. KEEP THE WORLD IN ONE GAMEPLAY SCENE

Continue using the unified gameplay scene architecture.

Do NOT return to loading separate Stable and Race scenes.

The Stable / Farm / Training / Blacksmith should exist together inside the same gameplay world.

The Main Race track can be physically somewhere else in the same scene.

When entering Main Race gameplay, the camera is allowed to:

- Hard cut
- Fast Cinemachine blend
- Transition to a distant race camera

A scene load is NOT required.

The Stable does NOT need to physically show the full Main Race track.

The Main Race should feel like a separate special event.

---

# 3. NEW STABLE LAYOUT PHILOSOPHY

Create or reorganize the Stable area so the player can visually understand the different systems.

A useful approximate layout could be:

BLACKSMITH

↓

FARM — STABLE / HORSE — TRAINING TRACK

The exact physical layout can be adapted to the existing environment.

Important:

### TRAINING

Training track should be very close to the Stable.

The player should be able to see horses actually running while interacting with the Farm and Stable.

### FARM

Farm should be physically visible and interactable.

### BLACKSMITH

Blacksmith should remain near the Farm, preferably to its left if practical.

For Milestone 4.5 the Blacksmith is primarily a visual location.

---

# 4. TRAINING MUST BECOME PHYSICALLY VISIBLE

The current Training system should no longer feel like only a timer or progress bar.

Create a small physical Training Track next to the Stable.

Training horses should visibly run around / through this area.

The player should be able to see them while managing the Stable.

The visual purpose is:

**"My horses are actually training while I manage everything else."**

The Training Track does NOT need realistic racing AI.

Keep the movement simple.

Possible implementations:

- Waypoints
- Spline
- Closed loop path
- Simple track path following

Choose whatever best fits the existing project architecture.

Do NOT use expensive navigation or complicated racing AI.

---

# 5. TRAINING LOOP

Training still functions as automatic incremental income.

Example loop:

Horse begins training lap

↓

Horse physically runs around Training Track

↓

Horse completes lap

↓

Training reward is granted

↓

Horse immediately begins another lap

The physical horse movement should match the Training progress.

Do NOT have:

Visual horse finishes immediately

while

Training timer still has 5 seconds remaining.

The visual and gameplay progression should approximately correspond.

# 6. TRAINING HORSES

# 7. ADDITIONAL TRAINING HORSES MUST BE VISIBLE

This is extremely important.

Buying:

**+1 Training Horse**

must not only modify a number.

A new horse should physically appear on the Training Track.

The player should immediately see:

Before:

1 horse running

After:

2 horses running

Later:

4 horses running

This creates strong visual incremental progression.

Use existing horse assets.

Different colors/variants can be used if they already exist and are easy to reuse.

Do not create a complicated horse collection system.

Training Horses are production units, not collectible characters.

---

# 8. TRAINING HORSE POSITIONING

Multiple Training Horses should not overlap perfectly.

Offset their positions around the track.

Example:

Horse 1 starts at 0%

Horse 2 starts at 25%

Horse 3 starts at 50%

Horse 4 starts at 75%

This should make the Training Track constantly feel alive.

They do not need sophisticated overtaking behaviour.

Simple looping is enough.

# 9. TRAINING UPGRADES

Create / rework Training progression around THREE clear upgrades.

## TRAINING HORSE COUNT

Adds another visible Training Horse.

Effects:

- More horses physically appear
- Training production increases

This should be one of the more expensive Training upgrades.

---

## TRAINING SPEED

Increases the movement speed of all Training Horses.

The visual horses must actually run faster.

Do NOT only reduce a hidden timer.

When the player upgrades Training Speed:

The horses on the Training Track should visibly become faster.

This is an important visual progression mechanic.

---

## TRAINING REWARD

Increases the reward produced by Training.

Example:

Training Reward Lv.1

+30 Coins per Training completion

Later:

+45

+60

+80

Use the current economy architecture.

Keep values configurable.

---

# 10. TRAINING ECONOMY MODEL

Keep the Training calculation simple.

A possible model:

Each Training Horse independently completes laps.

Each completed lap generates a Training Reward.

Therefore:

More Horses

=

More simultaneous production.

Training Speed

=

More laps completed over time.

Training Reward

=

More Coins per completed lap.

This creates three very understandable incremental upgrade paths:

**Quantity**

**Speed**

**Value**

Avoid complicated formulas.

---

# 11. TRAINING REWARD VISUAL FEEDBACK

Whenever a horse completes a lap:

Give small visual feedback near the horse or Training Track.

Example:

+35 Coins

The number can:

- Float upward
- Fade out
- Slightly scale

Do not create a giant popup every few seconds.

Keep frequent Training feedback lightweight.

If several horses finish near the same time, feedback should remain readable.

---

# 12. REMOVE TRAINING TIMER AS THE MAIN PRESENTATION

A Training timer/progress bar can exist internally or as secondary information.

However:

The primary presentation should now be the horses actually running.

The player should understand Training progress by watching the track.

Do not make the UI progress bar the main focus anymore.

If a Training progress UI remains:

Keep it subtle.

# 13. TRAINING DURING MAIN RACE

The player's primary horse cannot logically be Training and Main Racing simultaneously.

However, additional Training Horses may still be training if the architecture allows it.

For simplicity in Milestone 4.5, choose ONE consistent rule.

Recommended:

### Main Race active:

Training production pauses.

### Main Race finishes:

Training resumes.

This preserves the current simple state logic.

Do NOT spend significant development time simulating different horse ownership states.

We can revisit this later.

# 18. MAIN FARM GIMMICK — CLICK TO GROW FASTER

The main active Farm interaction is:

The player can repeatedly interact with crops to push them toward the next stage faster.

+3 Growth

Do not require hundreds of clicks.

The player should feel like:

"I can wait..."

"I can actively grow this right now."
